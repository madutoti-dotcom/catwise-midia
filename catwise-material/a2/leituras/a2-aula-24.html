Lia’s Big Day

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

The night before her presentation, Lia couldn’t sleep. She was going to talk about her life in English for five minutes. “What if I forget everything?” she thought.

In the morning, she read her checklist: speak slowly, look at the audience, don’t read the slides. She practised one more time in front of the mirror, and then once more for her cat.

At the class, her hands were cold. But when she showed the first photo, of her grandmother in Belém, she smiled. She forgot she was nervous. She told the açaí story, and everybody laughed.

At the end, her classmate asked, “What are you going to call your restaurant?” Lia thought for a moment. “I think I’ll call it Vovó,” she said. “Grandma.” Everybody clapped. It was one of the best days of her life.

Glossary: couldn’t sleep = não conseguiu dormir · what if = e se · mirror = espelho · hands = mãos · clapped = aplaudiram

Never Too Old to Play

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Dona Lourdes is seventy-two and she plays volleyball three times a week. She started when she was sixty-five. “Before that, I only walked slowly to the bakery,” she laughs.

Her team is called The Golden Girls. They play on the beach in Vitória every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday morning. “We don’t play fast,” she says, “but we play well. And we laugh loudly!”

Last year, the team played against a team of university students. The students ran faster and jumped higher, but The Golden Girls played more carefully and won the game. “The young people were very surprised,” says Lourdes.

Her grandson Caio, who is sixteen, sometimes plays with them. “Grandma plays better than me,” he says. “And she gets up earlier than me, too!”

Glossary: team = time · loudly = alto · against = contra · jumped higher = pularam mais alto · won = venceram · surprised = surpresos

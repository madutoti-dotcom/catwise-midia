A Busy Monday

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

It is Monday morning at Brightline, and Carla has a lot of emails. The first one is from her manager, Rob: “Hi Carla, could you email our supplier in Chile, please? It’s urgent. We need the new prices today.” Carla writes to the supplier immediately.

At ten o’clock, Aiko from accounts comes to her desk. “Carla, can you book a meeting room for this afternoon? I have a call with the bank.” “Sure, no problem,” says Carla. She books Room 2 from three to four.

After lunch, Diego from IT asks, “Could you test the new website with me now?” “Sorry, I can’t right now,” says Carla. “I’m finishing the sales report for Rob. He needs it by Friday. Can we do it tomorrow morning?” “Of course,” says Diego. “There’s no hurry.”

At five o’clock, Rob sends one more message: “Can you update the client list? Next week is fine.” Carla smiles, writes it on her list for next Monday and goes home.

Glossary: supplier = fornecedor · prices = preços · book = reservar · sales report = relatório de vendas · by Friday = até sexta · update = atualizar

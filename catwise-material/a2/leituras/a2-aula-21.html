How Lia Planned Her Talk

When Lia’s English teacher told her about the final project, she was nervous. “Talk about my life for five minutes? In English?” But then she made a plan.

First, she wrote a timeline on a big piece of paper: eight important years, from 1990, when she was born, to next year, when she wants to open her restaurant.

Then she looked for photos. She found one of her and her grandmother in Belém, one of her first day as a chef, and one of a plate of pasta she made in a cooking course.

After that, she chose three stories: one from her childhood, one about a big change, and one about her dream. “I can’t say everything,” she thought, “so I’m going to choose the best parts.”

In the end, she wrote her first slide: “My Life Story, by Lia. From the river to the kitchen.” She liked it. She wasn’t so nervous now.

Glossary: nervous = nervosa · piece of paper = folha de papel · looked for = procurou · course = curso · thought = pensou

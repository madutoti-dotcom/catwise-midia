Carla’s Interview

One year ago, Carla had an interview at Brightline. She arrived fifteen minutes early, and she was very nervous. Rob, the sales manager, smiled and asked the first question: “Tell me about yourself.”

“My name is Carla Dias. I’m 27, and I live in Curitiba. I studied business at university, and I worked as a shop assistant in a bookshop for three years.” “What did you do there?” asked Rob. “I helped customers, I organised the shelves, and I answered emails from online clients.”

“What are you good at?” “I’m good at talking to customers and solving problems. A good example is this: last year, a customer wanted a very rare book for his father’s birthday. I called five shops and found it in two days. He was so happy that he sent us flowers!”

Rob laughed. “And why do you want this job?” “I want to learn about logistics, and I want to use my English every day.” The next morning, Rob called her: “Carla, when can you start?”

Glossary: early = adiantada · shop assistant = vendedora de loja · bookshop = livraria · shelves = prateleiras · rare = raro · flowers = flores

Grandma’s Kitchen

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

When I was a child, I spent every Sunday at my grandmother’s house in Minas Gerais. Her kitchen was small, but it always smelled of coffee and cake.

My grandmother didn’t use recipes. She used her hands and her memory. For her famous banana cake, she put three eggs, a cup of sugar and a little butter in a big bowl. Then she added two cups of flour and a few very ripe bananas. “Not too much sugar,” she always said. “The bananas are sweet.”

While the cake was in the oven, she made a pot of coffee and cut a loaf of bread for us. There was always some cheese on the table, but there wasn’t any soda. “Soda is for parties,” she said.

Today, I make her cake every Sunday. I don’t have her recipe, but I have her words: a little butter, a few bananas, and not too much sugar.

Glossary: smelled of = tinha cheiro de · recipes = receitas · flour = farinha · ripe = maduras · oven = forno · pot = bule

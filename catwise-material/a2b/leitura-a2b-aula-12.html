Carla’s Tuesday

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

It is Tuesday, and Carla has a full day. At nine o’clock, a new client, Ms. Prado from NorteLog, visits the office. “Good morning, Ms. Prado. I’m Carla Dias. I work in customer service. Welcome to Brightline!” Carla presents the company: “We’re based in Curitiba, and we were founded in 2009. We deliver about 14,000 packages a month.”

At half past eleven, the phone rings. A client is not happy: his delivery is two days late. “I’m so sorry about that,” says Carla. “I’ll check with the driver and call you back in ten minutes.” She calls back with good news: the package will arrive at four o’clock.

At two o’clock, Carla speaks at the team meeting. “Three things today. First, Marina is starting on Monday. Next, the second floor will be closed next week. Finally, everyone has to change their password by Friday. Any questions?”

At four o’clock, she writes one last email: “Dear Ms. Prado, I am writing to confirm our meeting on Thursday at 10 a.m. Please find attached our price list. Best regards, Carla Dias.” One year ago, she was nervous in her interview. Today, she does everything in English.

Glossary: full day = dia cheio · welcome = bem-vinda · driver = motorista · rings = toca · price list = tabela de preços

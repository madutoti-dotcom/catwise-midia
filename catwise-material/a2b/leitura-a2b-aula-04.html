The Missed Call

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

On Wednesday morning, the phone rang at Brightline. Aiko was at the dentist, so Carla answered. “Good morning, Brightline. Carla speaking.”

“Hello, this is Helen Brooks from Atlas Bank. Can I speak to Aiko Sato, please?” “I’m afraid she’s out of the office this morning. Can I take a message?” “Yes, please. It’s about the payment for invoice 4471. There is a problem with the date. Could you ask her to call me back before four o’clock today?”

“Of course. Could I have your number?” “It’s 0161 496 0182.” Carla wrote the number quickly. “Let me read that back: 0161 496 0128.” “No, sorry. It’s 0182, not 0128.” “0182. Thank you, Ms. Brooks. I’ll tell her you called.”

Aiko came back at two o’clock and found the message on her desk. She called the bank at half past two, and they fixed the problem in ten minutes. “Thanks, Carla,” she said. “You read the number back. That saved my day!”

Glossary: rang = tocou · invoice = fatura, nota · payment = pagamento · call back = retornar a ligação · fixed = resolveram

Three Emails Before Lunch

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Carla has three emails to write before lunch. She makes a coffee and starts.

The first email is to Ms. Prado, a new supplier. Carla does not know her, so she writes carefully: “Dear Ms. Prado, I am writing to ask for your price list for 2027. Could you send it by Thursday, please? Thank you in advance. Best regards, Carla Dias.” She reads it twice and clicks “send”.

The second email is to Diego from IT. They are friends, so it is short: “Hi Diego, can you help me with the projector in Room 2 at 2 p.m.? I’m presenting to a client and I’m a bit nervous! Thanks, Carla.”

The third email is to Mr. Okafor from GreenBox Foods: “Dear Mr. Okafor, I am writing to confirm our meeting on Tuesday at 10 a.m. at our office. Please find attached the address and the agenda. Kind regards, Carla Dias.” Oh no! She forgot the attachment. She sends a second email: “Sorry, here is the attachment.” Then she goes to lunch.

Glossary: supplier = fornecedor · carefully = com cuidado · thank you in advance = desde já agradeço · attached / attachment = em anexo / anexo · agenda = pauta

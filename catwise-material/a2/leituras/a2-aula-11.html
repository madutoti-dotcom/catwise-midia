Learning at Forty

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Sandra is forty and she’s studying again. She works in a bank during the day, and three nights a week she’s taking a course in graphic design. “I always wanted to study design,” she says. “Now my children are older, so it’s my time.”

Next week is very busy. On Monday she’s working until six and her class starts at seven. On Wednesday she isn’t going to class: she’s taking her son to the doctor. “I’m sending my homework to the teacher on Tuesday night,” she says.

On Saturday morning she’s doing a test. She’s a little nervous, but she’s ready. And on Sunday? “On Sunday I’m having lunch with my parents. My mother is making lasagna. No computers, no tests!”

Sandra thinks it’s never too late to learn. “My classmates are twenty years younger than me,” she laughs, “but I’m the only one who does the homework!”

Glossary: course = curso · graphic design = design gráfico · ready = preparada · classmates = colegas de turma · never too late = nunca é tarde demais

After A2: What Next?

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Congratulations! You can now talk about your past, your experiences and your future in English. That’s a big step. But what’s next?

First, don’t stop. A language is like a muscle: if you don’t use it, it gets weaker. Try to use English a little every day, even for ten minutes. Short and often is better than long and rarely.

Second, choose things you enjoy. If you love cooking, watch cooking videos in English. If you love football, read the news about your team in English. You will learn more because you will want to continue.

Third, don’t be afraid of mistakes. Everybody makes mistakes, even advanced students. Mistakes show that you are trying.

In B1, you are going to tell longer stories, talk about your feelings and understand more of the films and series you love. We can’t wait!

Glossary: step = passo · muscle = músculo · weaker = mais fraco · often = com frequência · rarely = raramente · afraid of = com medo de

Cinema or Sofa?

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Every month, the magazine Weekend asks two readers a question. This month’s question: is it better to watch films at the cinema or at home?

Fernanda, 29: “I love going to the cinema. I enjoy the big screen, the sound and the popcorn. At home, I always look at my phone, but at the cinema I really watch the film. I think some films, like action films, are made for the cinema.”

Rafael, 45: “I don’t agree with Fernanda. Tickets are too expensive, and I hate queuing. At home I can stop the film, make a coffee and watch in my pyjamas. I’d like to go to the cinema more, but with three children, it isn’t cheap!”

And you? Write to us. The best answer wins two free cinema tickets. Rafael says he would like to win them.

Glossary: readers = leitores · screen = tela · queuing = ficar na fila · pyjamas = pijama · wins = ganha · free = grátis

Brazilian Inventions

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Do you know that some famous things were invented by Brazilians? The most famous example is the 14-bis, a plane that was flown by Alberto Santos-Dumont in Paris in 1906. Many Brazilians say he invented the aeroplane.

Many Brazilians also say that the caller ID on your phone was invented in Brazil, by Nélio Nicolai. And the electronic voting machine, which is used in all Brazilian elections, was created in Brazil in the 1990s.

Some inventions are more delicious. The brigadeiro was created in Brazil in the 1940s. Today it is made in thousands of houses for every birthday party.

Of course, not everybody agrees about who invented what. People in other countries tell different stories. But one thing is sure: good ideas are made everywhere.

Glossary: flown = pilotado · caller ID = identificador de chamadas · elections = eleições · voting machine = urna eletrônica · thousands = milhares · agrees = concorda

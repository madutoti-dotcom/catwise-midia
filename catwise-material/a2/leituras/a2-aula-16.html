The Plastic-Free Month

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Last July, the Ramos family tried a challenge: one month without plastic. “We had too much plastic in our rubbish,” says Júlia, the mother. “Every week there were too many bags. We wanted to change.”

The first week was difficult. They had to buy fruit and vegetables at the market, because at the supermarket everything was in plastic. They had to take their own bags and bottles everywhere.

The children had the most difficult job: no plastic toys and no sweets in plastic packets. “That wasn’t fair,” says Gabriel, eight. “But I found sweets in paper at the bakery!”

At the end of the month, their rubbish was very small. They didn’t stop using all plastic, but now they don’t have to think about it: they always take a bag to the shops. “It isn’t enough to save the planet,” says Júlia, “but it’s a start.”

Glossary: challenge = desafio · without = sem · their own = os próprios · packets = pacotes · fair = justo · save = salvar

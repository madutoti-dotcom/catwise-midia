My First Week at Brightline

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Hi! My name is Carla, and I am the new customer service assistant at Brightline, a small delivery company in Curitiba. I started last Monday. I answer calls and emails from clients, and I help them with late or lost packages.

On my first day, my manager, Rob, showed me the office. Rob is the sales manager. He works on the second floor and deals with our biggest clients. He is very friendly, but he is always on the phone!

Next to the kitchen, I met Aiko. She works in accounts and is responsible for invoices. If a client asks about a payment, I send the question to her. Then I met Diego from IT. He takes care of our laptops and passwords. On Tuesday, I forgot my password, and he helped me in two minutes.

It was a busy week, but I like my new team. Next week, I start my first project: a new FAQ page for our website.

Glossary: delivery company = transportadora · packages = encomendas · deals with = cuida de, trata com · invoices = notas fiscais, faturas · takes care of = cuida de · FAQ = perguntas frequentes

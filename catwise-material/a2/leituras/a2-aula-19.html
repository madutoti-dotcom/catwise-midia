The Wrong Suitcase

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Last December, Paulo flew from Manaus to São Paulo for his cousin’s wedding. The flight was longer than he expected, and he was very tired when he arrived.

While he was waiting at the baggage belt, he saw a black suitcase. It looked like his, so he took it and went to the hotel. When he opened it, there weren’t any shirts inside, only a lot of books and a few dresses. It was the wrong suitcase!

He called the airport. “Don’t worry,” said a woman. “Another passenger has your bag. She will bring it to the airport tomorrow morning.” But the wedding was that evening!

Paulo went to the nearest shop and bought the cheapest suit in the store. It was too big, but he went to the wedding. Next time, he says, he’s going to put a big red ribbon on his suitcase.

Glossary: flew = voou · baggage belt = esteira de bagagem · looked like = parecia · wrong = errada · suit = terno · ribbon = fita

From: Carla · To: Brightline team · Subject: Notes from Monday’s meeting

Hi everyone, here are the notes from this morning’s meeting.

First, a new colleague. Marina Lopes is joining the sales team. She will start on Monday, 3 June, and she will sit next to me. Please say hello!

Next, the office. The second floor will be closed from 10 to 14 June because the company is painting the walls and changing the lights. Everyone on the second floor will work from home that week. You don’t have to bring your computer back: Diego will collect the laptops on Friday, 7 June.

Finally, passwords. Diego is installing a new security system. Everyone has to change their password by Friday. You can do it in two minutes on the IT page.

Any questions? Write to me or to Rob. Best, Carla

Glossary: is joining = vai entrar na equipe · walls = paredes · collect = recolher · security system = sistema de segurança · by Friday = até sexta

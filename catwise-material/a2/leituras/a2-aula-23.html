Lia: Then and Now

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

When Lia was twenty, she moved to Recife to study. She became an English teacher and taught teenagers for eight years. “I liked teaching,” she says, “but I was always thinking about food.”

In 2018, she made the biggest decision of her life: she left her job and went to cooking school in São Paulo. Her family was worried. “It was the most difficult year of my life,” she remembers. “I had less money, and I worked harder than ever.”

Since then, she has worked in four restaurants. She has cooked for a famous actor, and she has won a small cooking competition. She has never been to Italy, but she has made pasta every day for five years.

“Ten years ago, I was shy and I didn’t believe in myself,” says Lia. “Now I’m more confident. My life is busier, but it’s much happier.”

Glossary: became = tornou-se · teenagers = adolescentes · worried = preocupada · less = menos · competition = competição · believe in myself = acreditar em mim

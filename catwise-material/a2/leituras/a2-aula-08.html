Next Year, New Me

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Every December, my friend Marcos makes a list of plans for the new year. This year, his list is very long.

“First, I’m going to start running,” he says. “Three times a week, in the park, at six in the morning.” I laugh. Marcos never gets up before eight. “It will be hard,” I say. “I know,” he answers. “I’ll be tired, but I’ll be healthy.”

His second plan is to learn to cook. “I’m going to take a cooking class on Saturdays. My mother will laugh, because I can’t even make rice.”

His third plan is the best one: he is going to visit Portugal in October with his sister. They already have the tickets.

I don’t think he will run every week, but I think he will love Portugal. And maybe, one day, he will cook rice for his mother.

Glossary: list = lista · start = começar · healthy = saudável · even = nem mesmo · already = já · maybe = talvez

1. Did you understand?

Escolha a resposta certa.

  1. Why does the writer laugh about the running plan?
    a) Marcos is a fast runner.   b) Marcos never gets up before eight.   c) The park is closed.
  2. Which plan is already sure?
    a) the trip to Portugal   b) running   c) cooking rice
  3. What does the writer think?
    a) Marcos will run every week.   b) Marcos won’t go to Portugal.   c) Marcos will love Portugal.

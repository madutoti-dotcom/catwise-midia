At the Trade Fair

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Last week, Rob and Carla went to a logistics trade fair in São Paulo. It was Carla’s first business event, and she was a little nervous. “Don’t worry,” said Rob. “Smile, say your name and your job, and give them your card.”

At eleven o’clock, a man stopped at the Brightline stand. “Good morning. Let me introduce myself. I’m Daniel Okafor, the purchasing manager at GreenBox Foods.” “Nice to meet you, Mr. Okafor. I’m Carla Dias. I work in customer service at Brightline,” said Carla. “Please call me Daniel,” he said, and he gave her his card.

Daniel wanted information about deliveries to the north of Brazil. Carla did not have all the answers, so she said, “Could I have your email address? I’ll send you our prices today.” “Sure. It’s d dot okafor at greenbox hyphen foods dot com.” “Sorry, could you spell your surname?” “Of course: O-K-A-F-O-R.”

That afternoon, Carla sent the email. Two days later, Daniel called Rob: GreenBox Foods is now a new client.

Glossary: trade fair = feira de negócios · stand = estande · purchasing manager = gerente de compras · deliveries = entregas · surname = sobrenome

Saturday at the Market

Every Saturday morning, Paula and her son Theo go to the street market near their house. Paula likes the market because the fruit is fresh and it is cheaper than the supermarket.

This Saturday, Paula has a short list. “We need some tomatoes, a lot of oranges for juice, and some cheese,” she says. At the first stall, she asks, “How much are the tomatoes?” “Eight reais a kilo,” says the man. She buys two kilos.

Theo wants some strawberries, but there aren’t any today. “Sorry, no strawberries this week,” says the woman at the fruit stall. They buy a lot of oranges instead.

At the cheese stall, Paula doesn’t buy much cheese: only two hundred grams. It is very expensive. When they get home, Theo looks in the bags. “Mum, where is the bread?” They forgot it! “Well,” says Paula, “we don’t need any bread. We have a lot of oranges!”

Glossary: street market = feira · stall = barraca · fresh = fresco · strawberries = morangos · instead = em vez disso · forgot = esqueceram

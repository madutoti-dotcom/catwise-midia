Four Seasons in One Day

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

People say that Curitiba has four seasons in one day. Last month, my cousin Ana visited me from Fortaleza, and she learned this the hard way.

In the morning it was cold and foggy, only nine degrees. Ana put on two jumpers and a jacket. “Will it be cold all day?” she asked. “It might get warmer,” I said.

At noon the fog disappeared and it was sunny and hot: twenty-six degrees! Ana took off her jumpers and bought an ice cream. “This is like Fortaleza!” she said.

In the afternoon the wind started, and at four o’clock there was a big storm. We didn’t have an umbrella, so we ran into a café. At night, it was cold again.

Before she went to bed, Ana looked at the forecast for the next day. “It says it won’t rain tomorrow,” she said. “But it might snow!” I laughed. It never snows here… almost never.

Glossary: learned the hard way = aprendeu do jeito difícil · put on / took off = vestiu / tirou · noon = meio-dia · disappeared = sumiu · almost = quase

The Wrong Boxes

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

On Tuesday afternoon, Carla answered the phone. “Good afternoon, Brightline. Carla speaking.” “Hello, this is Daniel Okafor from GreenBox Foods. I’m afraid there’s a problem with our delivery.”

“Oh, I’m sorry to hear that, Daniel. What happened?” “We ordered 40 boxes of glass bottles, but 40 boxes of plastic cups arrived this morning.” Carla wrote everything down. “So you ordered glass bottles, and you received plastic cups. Is that right?” “Yes, that’s right. And we need the bottles this week.”

“I’m so sorry about that. It was our mistake. I’ll check with the warehouse now and call you back in thirty minutes.” Carla called the warehouse. The bottles were there, with the wrong label.

She called Daniel back. “Good news. We’ll deliver the bottles on Thursday morning, and we’ll collect the cups at the same time. You don’t have to pay for this delivery.” “Thank you, Carla. That’s all right, then.” After the call, Carla sent him an email to confirm the day and time.

Glossary: glass bottles = garrafas de vidro · plastic cups = copos de plástico · mistake = erro · warehouse = depósito · label = etiqueta · collect = recolher

1. Did you understand?

Escolha a resposta certa.

  1. What was the problem?
    a) The delivery was late.   b) The wrong product arrived.   c) The invoice was wrong.
  2. Where were the bottles?
    a) in the warehouse, with the wrong label   b) at another client   c) in the van
  3. What does Daniel have to pay for the new delivery?
    a) double   b) half   c) nothing

2. What did Carla do?

Put Carla’s steps in order: she offered a solution · she said sorry · she sent an email to confirm · she listened and repeated the problem.

3. Find it

Find in the text: 2 ways to say sorry, 3 sentences with I’ll / we’ll and 1 sentence with I’m afraid.

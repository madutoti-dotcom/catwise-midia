Our Family Firsts

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Every Christmas, my family plays a game at dinner. Everyone says one thing they have never done. This year, the answers were very funny.

My grandfather Zé is eighty-one. He has worked on a farm all his life, and he has never flown in a plane. “I have never needed a plane,” he says. “I have a horse!”

My aunt Rosa is the opposite. She has been to fifteen countries and she has lived in Italy. But she has never cooked a Christmas dinner. “I’ve always eaten at other people’s houses,” she laughs.

My cousin Davi is a runner. He has run a marathon, but he has never watched a full football match. And me? I have never seen snow. “Have you ever been to Gramado in winter?” asked my aunt. “No, never,” I said. “Then we’re going in July,” she answered.

Glossary: firsts = primeiras vezes · farm = fazenda · horse = cavalo · opposite = oposto · full match = jogo inteiro

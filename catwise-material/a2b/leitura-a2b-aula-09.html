Welcome to Sabor do Vale

Sabor do Vale is a food company based in Caxias do Sul, in the south of Brazil. It was founded in 1987 by Lúcia Bertoni, who made grape juice in her kitchen and sold it at the local market.

Today, the company makes grape juice and jam with fruit from 120 small farms in the region. It has 85 employees, and Lúcia’s daughter, Paula, is the director. “My mother still visits the factory every week,” says Paula. “She tastes the juice and tells us if it is good!”

Sabor do Vale’s main customers are supermarkets and hotels in the south of Brazil. Three years ago, the company started to sell online, and now it sends products to every state. Last year, it produced 2.4 million bottles of juice, and sales went up nine per cent.

Next year, the company will open a small shop and café next to the factory. Visitors will see how the juice is made and, of course, they can try it.

Glossary: grape juice = suco de uva · jam = geleia · farms = sítios, fazendas · tastes = prova · bottles = garrafas · try = experimentar

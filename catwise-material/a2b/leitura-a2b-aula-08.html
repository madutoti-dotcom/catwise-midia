Brightline in Numbers

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Brightline is a delivery company from Curitiba, in the south of Brazil. It started in 2009 with only three people and one small van. Today, the company has 115 employees and 42 vans.

Every month, Brightline delivers about 14,000 packages to more than 600 clients. Most clients are small online shops, but the biggest client is a supermarket chain. Ninety-eight per cent of the packages arrive on time. “The other two per cent are my job,” says Carla from customer service.

Last year was a good year. Sales went up 12.5 per cent, and the company opened a second office in Joinville. This year, Brightline is buying ten electric vans. They cost more than normal vans, but they use 60 per cent less energy.

What is next? Rob, the sales manager, wants 1,000 clients by 2028. “It’s a big number,” he says, “but in 2009 we had only five.”

Glossary: van = furgão · packages = encomendas · supermarket chain = rede de supermercados · on time = no prazo · went up = subiram · less = menos

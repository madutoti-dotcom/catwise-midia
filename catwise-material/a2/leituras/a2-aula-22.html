Lia’s Childhood

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

I was born in Belém in 1990, in a small house near the river. My mother was a nurse and my father worked at the port. They worked a lot, so I spent most days with my grandmother.

Her kitchen was my favourite place. While she was cooking, I sat on a little chair and watched. She cooked fish, rice with jambu and, of course, açaí.

When I was seven, I was helping her when I dropped a big pot of açaí on the floor. It was everywhere: on the floor, on the dog, on me! I started to cry, but my grandmother laughed. Then she taught me to make it again, step by step.

My grandmother died when I was fifteen, but I still remember her laugh. That’s why I love cooking today. Every time I cook, I’m in her kitchen again.

Glossary: port = porto · spent = passava · most days = a maioria dos dias · jambu = jambu (erva da Amazônia) · step by step = passo a passo · died = morreu

From: Rob · To: Sales and Accounts teams · Monday, 9:12
Hi everyone, we’re having our monthly meeting on Thursday at 3 p.m. in Room 1. Aiko is presenting the new invoice system. Please read the report before the meeting. Thanks, Rob

From: Aiko · To: Rob · Monday, 10:40
Hi Rob, I’m afraid I can’t make it on Thursday afternoon. I’m visiting the bank at 2:30. Can we move the meeting to Friday morning? Sorry about that! Aiko

From: Rob · To: Aiko · Monday, 11:05
No problem, Aiko. Does Friday at 10 work for you? Room 1 is busy in the morning, but Room 2 is free. Rob

From: Aiko · To: Rob · Monday, 11:20
Yes, that works. Thank you! Could you tell Diego and Carla? I’m sending the report to everyone today.

From: Rob · To: Sales and Accounts teams · Monday, 11:45
Hi everyone, a small change: our meeting is now on Friday at 10 a.m. in Room 2. See you there. Rob

Glossary: monthly = mensal · invoice system = sistema de faturas · I can’t make it = não vou conseguir ir · move = mudar · busy = ocupada

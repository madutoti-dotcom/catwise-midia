Lost in Lisbon

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Last summer, my husband and I went to Lisbon for the first time. On the second night, we wanted to have dinner at a famous fado restaurant, but our phones had no battery. We were lost.

We asked a man at a bus stop. “Excuse me, how do we get to the Casa do Fado?” He smiled. “It’s easy. Walk along the river for five minutes. Go past the big square, then turn right. Go up a small street, through a little park, and the restaurant is opposite a blue church.”

We walked along the river and went past the square. We turned right, but there were three small streets! We chose the wrong one and walked for twenty minutes.

Finally, an old woman helped us. She walked with us through the park and showed us the blue church. We arrived late, but the fado was beautiful. Now I always carry a paper map in my bag.

Glossary: lost = perdidos · battery = bateria · square = praça · chose = escolhemos · finally = finalmente · carry = carrego

1. Did you understand?

Escolha a resposta certa.

  1. Why couldn’t they use their phones?
    a) There was no internet.   b) They forgot them at the hotel.   c) The phones had no battery.
  2. What was the problem after they turned right?
    a) There were three small streets.   b) The park was closed.   c) It started to rain.
  3. What does the writer do now?
    a) She never travels.   b) She always carries a paper map.   c) She only takes taxis.

2. The way

Write the directions in order: river, square, right, small street, park, blue church. Use along, past, up, through, opposite.

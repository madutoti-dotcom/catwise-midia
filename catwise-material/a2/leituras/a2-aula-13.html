The Series Club

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

My friends and I have a WhatsApp group called The Series Club. Every Monday, we choose a series and everyone watches one episode a day. On Sunday, we meet at a café and talk about it.

This week was a disaster. On Monday, Carla said, “I’ve already watched the whole series! I watched it all on Saturday night.” Nobody was happy.

Pedro hasn’t started it yet. “I’ve just finished a very long book,” he said. “I’ll start tomorrow.” And Bia? Bia has watched three episodes, but she fell asleep in the second one, so she doesn’t know what happened.

Me? I watched every episode, one a day, like a good member. On Sunday we went to the café. Carla told us the end, Pedro covered his ears, and Bia asked, “Who is the man with the hat?” Next week, we’re choosing a comedy.

Glossary: whole = inteira · disaster = desastre · nobody = ninguém · fell asleep = pegou no sono · covered his ears = tapou os ouvidos

  2. Why hasn’t Pedro started the series?
    a) He doesn’t like series.   b) His phone broke.   c) He has just finished a long book.
  3. What problem does Bia have?
    a) She fell asleep, so she doesn’t know what happened.   b) She watched it twice.   c) She lost the WhatsApp group.

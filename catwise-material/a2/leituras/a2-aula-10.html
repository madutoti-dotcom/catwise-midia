Ask Dr. Lima

Dear Dr. Lima, I’m 42 and I work in an office. I’m always tired, I have headaches in the afternoon, and I don’t have time for the gym. What should I do? — Tired Tânia

Dear Tânia, thank you for your letter. A lot of people have the same problem. First, you don’t have to go to the gym. You should walk for twenty minutes a day: to the bakery, at lunchtime, or with a friend after work.

Second, how much water do you drink? Many headaches come from not drinking enough water. You should drink a bottle of water in the morning and another one in the afternoon.

Third, sleep. You should go to bed before eleven o’clock, and you mustn’t use your phone in bed. The light from the screen keeps your brain awake.

If the headaches don’t stop in two weeks, you must see a doctor. Small changes can make a big difference. — Dr. Lima

Glossary: same = mesmo · lunchtime = hora do almoço · enough = suficiente · screen = tela · awake = acordado · difference = diferença

A Year in Dublin

Como ler com o Sluka: 1) Instale o Sluka grátis no Chrome: sluka.app. 2) Escolha inglês. 3) Abra esta página no Chrome. As palavras novas aparecem destacadas; aponte para a palavra para ver o significado. Leia uma vez inteira e depois leia de novo em voz alta.

Dear Ana,

I’ve been in Dublin for almost a year now, and I’ve learned so much! My English is better than before, and I speak more confidently at work. I have to speak English all day, so I’ve had no choice!

Have you ever eaten a full Irish breakfast? I tried it in my first week. It was delicious, but it was too much food for me. You should try it when you come.

The weather is the hardest part. It rains a lot, so you mustn’t forget your umbrella. But the people are very friendly, and the city is full of music. Last week I went to a concert in a pub that was built in 1198!

Next month I’m flying to Brazil for my sister’s wedding. Would you like to meet for coffee? I’d love to see you. Love, Carol

Glossary: almost = quase · no choice = sem escolha · full Irish breakfast = café da manhã irlandês completo · hardest = mais difícil · pub = bar tradicional
